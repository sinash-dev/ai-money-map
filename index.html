How to read the map

→

Arrows follow the money

Every line points from the company that pays to the company that receives. An investment points from investor to startup. A compute contract points from the buyer (say, OpenAI) to the seller (say, Oracle). The moving dots show the direction.

A loop is money that comes back: A pays B and B pays A, directly or through other companies.

✓

How real is each number?

Completed: the money has moved. Signed: contracted, often paid over years. Letter of intent or paused: a promise that may never happen, drawn dashed.

Amounts are marked disclosed, reported (press, not the company) or estimated (our split of a round). "Up to" deals are counted at their maximum. Use the filters to count only binding money.

◎

Coverage and limits

This tracks the largest AI deals since 2019 across the US, China, Europe, the Middle East and Asia. It doesn't cover every startup round. When a round's investors aren't split out, the money is grouped as "VCs, PE and other funds", with the named investors listed.

Not investment advice. Spotted an error or a missing deal? Open an issue on GitHub.
